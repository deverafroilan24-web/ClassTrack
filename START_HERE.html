ClassTrack Quick Start

Automated AI Classroom Participation & Recitation Tracking

Cloud Web Application

🌐 1. Teacher Dashboard

No downloads or software installation required! Works on any phone, tablet, iPad, or computer browser.

  • Open the cloud link in Chrome, Safari, or Edge.
  • Select your class section (e.g., BSIT-3).
  • Click "Start Class Session" to begin recitation monitoring.
  • Tap "+1 Point" when students answer, or "End Session" to export CSV grades.

Windows Desktop App

📷 2. Classroom Camera

Runs YOLOv11 pose detection on the classroom PC and automatically sends raised hands to the live dashboard.

  • Locate and double-click ClassTrack_Setup_v1.0.exe.
  • Click Next → Install (no Python or coding needed).
  • Launch ClassTrack from your Windows Desktop.
  • The camera window will open with real-time pose HUD overlay!
Run ClassTrack Setup (.exe)
⚠️ Windows SmartScreen notice: If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", simply click "More info" → "Run anyway".

Frequently Asked Questions

Do non-technical users need to install Python?
No! The installer (ClassTrack_Setup_v1.0.exe) bundles Python, PyTorch, YOLOv11, OpenCV, and AI model weights. Everything is 100% self-contained.
Can teachers access the dashboard from their smartphone?
Yes! Simply open https://classtrack-mm41.onrender.com on your iPhone, Android, iPad, or laptop. No downloads necessary.
How do I test hand tracking at home?
1. Open the Web Dashboard on your browser and click "Start Class Session".
2. Launch the Desktop Camera app and point your webcam at yourself.
3. Raise your hand high — the camera HUD turns green, and your seat immediately pops up at the top of the recitation queue on the web dashboard!
